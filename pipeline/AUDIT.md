# Audit of DOCTRINE.md, RESEARCH.md and template/

Independent audit, 12 Nov 2026. Scope: DOCTRINE.md (D1 to D14 and the playbook table), the hand-written parts of RESEARCH.md, the eleven topic notes in research/, template/, and the three playbooks in `terminal:nvim/`. Twelve primary sources were fetched. `measure.py` was read, its self-test was run, and it was exercised in a scratch directory outside the workspace.

## Verdict

Most numbers the doctrine leans on are copied faithfully. All twelve primary sources I opened match their ledger rows on the key figures. Decisions D4, D5 and the statistical core of D6 hold up well.

The serious problems are elsewhere:
- **Isolation dropped.** The doctrine quietly drops the agent-isolation controls that the security note rates high confidence and that two playbooks already required, while the table claims the rule was "Kept, tightened".
- **Wrong noise floor.** The template's `measure.py` silently reports a wrong A/A noise floor in its own documented pilot-then-main workflow.
- **Overstated confidence.** Several verdicts (D2, D8 "short", dropping handoff rituals and all idea mechanisms) read as settled even though the notes rate their evidence low to medium, or the cited rows say something narrower.
- **Missing tooling.** The template and D9 assume `make` targets and scripts that do not exist yet, and D13 lists them as future work.

## What holds up

- **D4, one writer per unit.** H08 and H10 are verified. The note's contrary cases (H04 to H06) are acknowledged, with their budgets.
- **D5, an agent's "done" is not evidence.** E20, E11 and E12 support it. The ledger numbers are verified. The Against line honestly concedes that no controlled study supports a separate reviewer.
- **D6, the statistical core.** The coverage formula 1 − 2·0.5ⁿ and the exact sign test in `measure.py` are correct. The self-test passes all 8 checks. B07's 6 of 8 is verified, and the case against the old "median of 3" rule stands. The schedule shuffles arms inside each pair and is reproducible from a seed.
- **D7, re-measure loop winners and explore on free evaluators.** L03 is verified to the digit. The Against line includes Karpathy's counter-case.
- **D8, the mechanism.** `CLAUDE.md` = `@AGENTS.md` plus Claude-only lines, no generated files, no overviews. All are verified against C01 and C26.
- **D10, pinned hardware, `retries=0` and timeouts.** These match the notes and docs. Multiple accounts are correctly called a grey area, matching the verified terms.
- **D12, `Signed-off-by` by the human only.** Verified against the kernel text. The rule that the human writes the PR text is well supported by note 07.
- **D3, the division of labour.** Matches notes 03 and 09.

## Not checked

- **Ledger rows.** Not all of the 113 cited rows were checked against their notes; about 70 were. None of the 279 uncited ledger rows were checked, and neither was `build_research.py`.
- **Secondary-only primaries.** V22, V23, S13, S16 and R12 were not fetched; the notes say these pages were blocked or reached only through relays. The B07 "70+ runs" figure was not checked.
- **Credentials on this machine.** Whether agent sessions on this machine can reach push, `gh`, Modal or Hugging Face credentials (D11's "true today") was deliberately not inspected.
- **Agent behaviour.** Whether `claudeMdExcludes` would cover `vendor/` could not be checked, because no settings file exists. No agent runs were made.
- **Unclear files.** `pipeline/DECISIONS.md` appeared at 12:40, during the audit, and is outside the stated scope. The workspace's `opportunities/` and `upstream/` folders were not read.
- **The 393 versus 392 count.** A raw grep counts 393 ledger rows against RESEARCH's 392; one extra table row is probable, but this was not investigated.
